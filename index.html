Where It All Began

History of Consulting

Consulting is not a modern invention — it has evolved over a century to help organizations make better decisions. Today, we apply that legacy using R, SAS, AI, and machine learning.

Early 1900s — Foundations

Scientific management and early industrial advisors emerge, helping factories optimize operations and efficiency.

1930s–1950s — The Big Firms Rise

Strategy and management consulting firms form, specializing in corporate structure, accounting, and operational strategy.

1960s–1980s — The Analytics Era Begins

Statistical tools like SAS and R transform consulting, enabling evidence-based decision-making and data-driven strategy.

1990s–2010s — Digital Consulting

Technology, ERP systems, and data warehousing reshape consulting, connecting strategy directly to digital systems.

2020s — AI & Machine Learning

Consulting now integrates AI and machine learning to build predictive models, automate decisions, and reduce business risk at scale.
